# Evaluation cases

- Load: “I need to record a benchmark, its hypothesis and its result in our research corpus.”

- Task: “The control failed in my delivered investigation; record a verdict.” Expected: accept the delivered result first, then assess the hypothesis as inconclusive; do not claim support or refutation.
